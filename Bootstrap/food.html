Pakistani Foods

KARACHI BIRYANI

Karachi biryani is a fiery, aromatic rice dish defined by tender meat, tangy dried plums, and essential spiced potatoes.

LAHORI NIHARI

Lahori Nihari is a slow-cooked, rich beef or mutton stew infused with fragrant spices.It features melt-in-the-mouth meat and bone marrow simmered to velvety perfection.Served hot with crisp naan, it is a beloved culinary tradition in Pakistan.

PESHAWARI KABAB

Peshawari kabab, commonly known as chapli kabab, is a famous minced meat patty originating from Peshawar, Pakistan.

HYDERABADI BIRYANI

Hyderabadi biryani is a world-famous rice dish from India. It mixes fragrant basmati rice, tender meat (chicken or mutton), and bold spices. Cooks slow-cook it using the "dum" method with a sealed pot to trap rich aromas.

ISLAMABADI PULAO

Islamabadi pulao—famously styled by iconic spots like Savour Foods—is a uniquely comforting dish of yellow-hued, spice-infused rice served with tender roasted chicken and shami kebabs.

MULTANI HALWA

Multani Halwa, famous as Multani Sohan Halwa, is a rich, traditional Pakistani dessert. It features a dense, chewy texture and is packed with desi ghee and nuts.
